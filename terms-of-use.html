When inquiring about or applying for admission to our competitive examination coaching programs (including TNPSC Group 4, Group 2, RRB NTPC/Group D, Banking, SSC, Police & Defence, and TET/TRB), you agree to:

  • Provide true, accurate, current, and complete details regarding your identity, contact information, and educational qualifications.

  • Ensure that you meet the educational and age eligibility criteria prescribed by the respective official recruitment commissions for the exams you prepare for.

2. Course Admission, Batches & Timings

Admission to coaching courses at Sri RR Academy is subject to seat availability, verification of application details, and timely payment of fees:

  • Batch Allotment: Students are allocated to batches (Morning, Evening, Regular, or Weekend) based on availability and academic scheduling. Requests for batch transfers are subject to management approval.
  • Timetable & Schedule Adjustments: The Academy reserves the right to modify class timetables, adjust batch hours, conduct extra revision sessions, or schedule special mock tests to align with official government exam dates.
  • Faculty Assignment: Experienced faculty and subject experts are assigned at the discretion of the Academy management to provide optimal pedagogical support.

3. Fee Structure, Payment & Refund Policy

Important Notice on Course Fees & Refunds

Course fees must be paid in full or according to the agreed payment schedule prior to or at the commencement of coaching. Once admission is confirmed and batch classes have commenced, fees paid are non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.

  • Payment Confirmation: Official receipts are issued for all fees received. Students are advised to retain their fee receipts throughout the course duration.
  • Installment Schedules: Where installment facilities are approved, failure to pay subsequent installments by the due date may result in suspension of classroom access, test series portal privileges, or course materials.
  • Administrative Decisions: In rare cases of duplicate payment due to electronic gateway errors, refunds will be processed back to the original source method after administrative verification within 7-10 working days.
